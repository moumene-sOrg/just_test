RESCUE TIPS

Check Fences: Walk your perimeter to ensure there are no loose boards or gaps.

Pet-proof the Kitchen: Keep chemicals and toxic foods like chocolate out of reach.

Identify Local Vets: Save the number of the nearest 24/7 emergency clinic.
